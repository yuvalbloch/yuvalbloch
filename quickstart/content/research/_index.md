## Research

My M.Sc. research at **Ben-Gurion University of the Negev**, conducted within the *Ecological Complexity Lab* under the supervision of Prof. Shai Pilosof, modelled the effects of land-use change on the risk of tick-borne disease transmission in rural Madagascar. The completed thesis and a manuscript in review are listed on the [publications page](/publications/).

The work combines a stochastic, agent-based tick population model with a cellular-automata model of smallholder land-use change, and a set of multi-scale landscape metrics for comparing the resulting scenarios. The posts below follow that work in the order it was actually built — including the attempts that did not work.
